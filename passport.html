FabricPassport / Passport model
DPP · Textile data model

What belongs in a fabric passport?

A practical conceptual data model that separates identity, composition, provenance, manufacturing, sustainability, compliance and lifecycle information.

ID

Product identity

Passport ID, SKU, batch, article, product name, fabric type, issuer and version.

%

Composition

Fiber types, blend percentages, recycled content, material grade and relevant component breakdowns.

↗

Provenance

Material origin, suppliers, facilities, countries, production stages and chain-of-custody references.

⚙

Manufacturing

Spinning, weaving, knitting, dyeing, finishing, assembly, dates, lots and process attributes.

✓

Evidence & compliance

Certificates, test reports, declarations, verification status and references to source evidence.

♻

Lifecycle & circularity

Care, durability, repairability, reuse, recyclability, end-of-life instructions and material recovery.
