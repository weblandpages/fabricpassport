FabricPassport / Standards & regulation
Interoperability · DPP · Identifiers

Build for interoperability, not another data silo.

This page maps the main technical concepts a textile DPP implementation may need to consider. It is a conceptual overview, not legal advice or an official standards interpretation.

EU

Digital Product Passport context

Product information requirements, access rights, persistence and data carrier concepts form part of the broader EU DPP direction.

ID

Identifiers

Stable product, batch, facility and organization identifiers are foundational for linking data across systems.

API

Data exchange

APIs, structured schemas and event-based interfaces can reduce manual exchange and improve machine readability.

QR

Data carriers

QR codes or other carriers can resolve a physical product to an online passport endpoint.

ACL

Access control

Different actors may require public, partner, regulator or restricted views of the same underlying data.

✓

Verification

Claims become more valuable when linked to source evidence, test results, certifications or trusted verification processes.

Important: FabricPassport.com is an independent conceptual project. For implementation, verify current legal requirements and applicable standards with official sources and qualified specialists.
