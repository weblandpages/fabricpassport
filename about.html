About FabricPassport.com

FabricPassport.com is a conceptual website exploring how Digital Product Passports, material traceability and lifecycle information could be structured for fabrics and textile supply chains.

01

Purpose

Explain the information architecture, workflows and stakeholder views around textile DPP concepts.

02

Scope

Textiles, fabrics, fibers, manufacturing stages, provenance, evidence and circularity-related data.

03

Status

Conceptual and informational. It is not an operating certification body, official registry or EU service.

FabricPassport.com is also available for acquisition.
