FabricPassport / Use cases
One passport · Multiple stakeholders

The same passport serves different decisions.

A textile DPP should expose context-relevant information without forcing every stakeholder to consume the full internal data model.

M

Manufacturer

Connect source materials, production batches, process records and quality evidence.

B

Brand

Consolidate supplier data, product claims, composition and supporting documentation.

A

Auditor / verifier

Review evidence trails, declared attributes and links to certificates or test documentation.

R

Recycler

Identify fiber composition, blends, finishes and disassembly or recycling guidance.

C

Consumer

Access concise information about composition, origin, care, durability and end-of-life options.

P

Procurement

Compare supplier declarations, material attributes, traceability completeness and documentation.
