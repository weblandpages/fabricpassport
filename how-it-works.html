How a textile passport can work.

A conceptual workflow for collecting, validating, linking and publishing textile product data through a persistent digital identity.

01

Identify the material or product

Assign a stable identifier to a fabric, roll, batch, SKU or finished textile product.

02

Collect source data

Bring together composition, suppliers, facilities, process steps, certificates and supporting evidence.

03

Link transformations

Connect upstream materials to spinning, weaving or knitting, dyeing, finishing and assembly events.

04

Validate and govern

Apply data quality rules, provenance controls, permissions and verification references.

05

Publish the passport view

Expose the right information to brands, authorities, recyclers, business partners or consumers.
